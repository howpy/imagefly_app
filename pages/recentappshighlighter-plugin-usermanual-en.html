1. Introduction

Recent Apps Highlighter automatically monitors usage of the Prototype Apps menu and makes the N most recently used entries (default 3) instantly visible: the entries turn bold with a colored dot marker, and a pinned '★ recently used' block appears at the very top of the menu — clicking a ★ entry triggers the original. The more plugins you enable and the longer the menu grows, the more useful this gets.

This feature is provided by a third party (Prototype Labs / Apps), not officially developed or supported by Dragonfly. Pure in-process PyQt6 — no install/internet/GPU/venv.

2. How it works

  • Auto-starts with Dragonfly: once the plugin loads it finds the Prototype Apps menu and hooks every entry — no need to open the settings panel first.
  • Additive only: monitoring attaches an extra recorder to each menu entry's trigger signal; Dragonfly's own menu behavior is completely untouched.
  • History survives restarts: every click is recorded to %LOCALAPPDATA%\DragonflyPrototypeLabs\RecentApps\usage.json (last 500 events kept).
  • Safe across the 中文/English toggle: entries are keyed internally by their English source titles, so history stays valid after switching the UI language, and the ★ block shows titles in the current language.

3. The settings panel

Menu Prototype Apps ▸ Recent Apps Highlighter… opens the settings & history panel:

SettingMeaning

Pinned block at the topinserts the '★ recently used' shortcut block at the very top of the menu
Background color (experimental)tries to paint the entry background; Dragonfly's dark theme may prevent it from showing — the other cues are unaffected

4. How to use

1. Just use any tool in the Prototype Apps menu — monitoring happens automatically, nothing to configure.

2. Open the Prototype Apps menu again: the tools you just used are bold with a dot, and appear in the '★ recently used' block at the top.

3. To adjust (count, toggles, clear history), open Prototype Apps ▸ Recent Apps Highlighter….

5. Requirements

No install/internet/GPU/venv. Only the Prototype Apps menu itself is monitored (launches via the App Store's Launch button etc. are not counted).
